## Notes

- **Images:** Use vertical 2:3 ratio images (1000×1500px) for best reach. Canva has free pet templates.
- **Hashtags:** Include 3–5 hashtags per pin from the descriptions file.
- **Re-pinning:** It's fine to re-pin the same URL to different boards — Pinterest counts each board save separately.
- **Week 5+:** Rotate back to Week 1 content and add any new blog posts published by the weekly cron.
- **Analytics:** Check Pinterest Analytics after 30 days. Double down on the top 5 performing pins.
